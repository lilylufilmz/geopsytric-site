Features

Made for people who see patterns everywhere

Built by a tattoo artist, for tattoo artists, sacred geometry lovers and everyone who gets lost in symmetry.

Any fold you want

3, 6, 12, 24, 64. Mirror on or off, several centres on one canvas. Other apps stop at 8. This one doesn't.

All 17 tile patterns

Every way a pattern can repeat on a flat surface. Bands and sleeves that wrap without a seam.

Stencil ready

One tap to clean black lines. PDF and SVG at true size in millimetres, big designs over several A4 sheets.

Grow

Hit one button and your pattern has kids. Pick the best one, grow it again. You stay the artist.

Neon and music

Glowing brushes, colour cycles, and a music mode where the pattern pulses with the beat.

Loops for Reels

Make it breathe, spin and shift hue, then export a seamless video for Reels and TikTok.
