FAQ

Questions

The short answers.

When can I get it?

GeoPSYtric comes to the App Store first, Google Play follows. Get early access and you hear it first.

Does it work on my iPad?

Yes, and Apple Pencil pressure works.

Do I need an account?

No. Your drawings stay on your device. No tracking, no ads.

Can I use the designs for paid tattoos?

Yes. What you draw is yours.

Will the stencil print at the right size?

Yes. PDF and SVG export in real millimetres. Designs wider than 190 mm come out on several A4 sheets with crop marks.

Does it work offline?

Yes, everything runs on your device.

Refunds?

You buy GeoPSYtric through the App Store or Google Play, so refunds go through their standard process. On iPhone and iPad, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, follow Google Play's refund help.
